I know things between us did not go well in the last few weeks, but I just want you to have a look at this small creation.

A girl from a different place gets into a college in a different state which was completely new to her. She didn't know how to make things right; she was facing a lot of trauma because of her parents. But she never let it come across her way to success—she still fights it with maturity. She is very talented, blessed with music, dance, and what not. But at the same time, she is annoying, rowdy... but cute.

A boy from UP who has anger issues. A foodie who just lives to eat and enjoy. Suffering from poor luck, his destiny landed him in a different space. With a habit of getting into everything to pursue the title of "Jack of All Trades."

अध्याय 1: पहली मुलाकात

They went on their first college trip to Ahmedabad. Fatty had grown a new habit of capturing images—he wanted to become the best photographer of India. While trying to click one such masterpiece, their paths crossed. The girl asked him, "Are you alright?" The answer that came made her spit the fifth Marie Gold biscuit from the pack onto the guy's face. Fatty said, "I have a passion for photography." Stupid Fatty was unaware that the spit which landed on his face went deep inside and touched his heart for the first time.

अध्याय 2: शाम की सैर और सिलसिला

Fatty approached the girl for a walk, and then they started doing it frequently. The evening walks became a cherished routine, where conversations flowed effortlessly and silences felt comfortable.

अध्याय 3: चाँद के नीचे किया अपने चाँद से प्यार का इज़हार

The boy confessed his love for her. The girl was shocked, but this feeling which was new to her made her feel special. They kissed under the moon, sealing a moment that would remain etched in their hearts forever.

अध्याय 4: दोनों के बीच के हालातों से मजबूर होना पड़ा एक दूसरे से दूर

The circumstances were not in their favor, and they got separated. Both of them were broken. But the guy, whose feelings were true, shattered into infinite pieces. At the same time, he faced family problems, a death, and what not. Completely broken, he fell into depression when it felt like everything had ended. Yet, he chose to rise above all the miseries and fought his way out.

अध्याय 5: एक अनचाहा एहसास

Being away from the guy, the girl realized his value in her life. She felt how his company made her feel safe. She felt how his mischiefs were like entertainment that made her want to mother him. She missed every inch of him—the hugs, the kisses, the walks, everything.

अध्याय 6: पुनः मिलन

The girl had developed very strong feelings toward the man. She requested him to give her a chance again, and the man agreed. But this time, he was very cautious. The girl put in a lot of effort to win him back, but the man, with his wounds, flashbacks, and ego, was moving gradually. Disagreements started again, but this time the girl kept her patience, wanting to sort things out between them.

अध्याय 7: मिट्टी के मकान

Things between them started going smoothly, all because of the girl's efforts. The man's attitude was getting lenient, but at a pace which the girl couldn't wait for. Slowly, their fragile foundation was being rebuilt.

अध्याय 8: समय और सुध-बुध का खेल निराला, दोहरा डाला इतिहास दोबारा

The girl, wanting to hold the man's hand, was tired of everything. She couldn't do it anymore; the urge to win him was gone. Numbness took over her. But the man had already lit the old, rusted lamp of love inside him and wanted to illuminate it on her to make her feel special and loved once again. But it was too late.

प्रायश्चित

त्रिदीप्शा, मुझे एक मौका देना या ना देना तुम्हारे हाथ में है। मुझे मालूम है कि आपके सब्र का बांध अब टूट चुका है और अब आपसे नहीं पाएगा। मैं भी इसमें आप पर दबाव नहीं डालना चाहता। पर बिना कुछ किए ऐसे हार मान लेना मैंने नहीं सीखा है। चूक होना इंसानी फितरत में होता है, पर उन गलतियों से सीखना उसकी जिम्मेदारी। मैं बस आपसे माफी मांगना चाहता हूं अपने गुनाहों की और आपको इस कदर सताने की। आप इन सब चीजों से बेहतर डिजर्व करते हो। हो सके तो मुझे माफ कर दो।

मुझे पता है आप इस समय मानसिक स्थिति में नहीं हो कि हमारे बारे में सोचो, पर आपका लिया हुआ फैसला हमारा आगे का रिश्ता कैसा होगा वो तय करेगा। मैं बिल्कुल समझता हूं इस मोड़ पर आके आपको इस रिश्ते में और ऊर्जा देने का कोई मन नहीं है।

मैं आपसे कुछ नहीं मांगता, मांगता हूं तो बस एक मौका। क्या आप वो मौका हमारे इस रिश्ते को दोगे?

अध्याय 9: प्यार की ईंटों से बना नया रिश्तों का महल

आपने ये मौका देके मुझपे बहुत अहसान किया है, मैं आपको कोई भी शिकायत का मौका नहीं दूंगा। मैं उम्मीद करता हूं कि इस नए महल में सिर्फ और सिर्फ हमारा प्यार घर करे और भगवान हमें हर प्रकार की नज़र और परेशानियों से बचा के रखे। मैं आपको बता नहीं सकता कि आपको अपने बाहों में भरके जो सुख मिलता है वो अपनी प्यारी माँ के पर दबाने जितना अनमोल है।

अध्याय 9: अलविदा

आपके संग बिताया हुआ हर एक लम्हा मेरे लिए खास है, आपको अपने बाहों में भरना मैं बहुत मिस करूंगा। आपके कानों में लगा वो फूल, जिससे उस फूल की रौनक बढ़ जाती है, वो लम्हा मैं मिस करूंगा। अलविदा मेरी त्रिदीप्शा।
